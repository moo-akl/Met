---
name: Hotspot QR marketing strategy
description: User's expansion of venue QR acquisition to tourist attractions and city hotspots.
---

The user plans to create venues for tourist attractions and hotspots in all cities and print QR codes at those locations. Scanning should lead to a smooth, easy, quick check-in.

**Why:** The user stated their marketing plan changed from the earlier venue-focused approach to this hotspot rollout.

**How to apply:** Evaluate QR acquisition for existing signed-in users, installed-but-signed-out users, and first-time installers. Do not assume a staffed business is available to help visitors complete a scan.

Allow authenticated new users to complete a private check-in before adding a profile photo. Require a completed profile and photo checks before discoverability or social interactions; check-in must not automatically enable public visibility.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose “Before social features, not private check-in” for the photo requirement.

**How to apply:** Separate private visit recording from social eligibility, enforce the restrictions server-side, and preserve the scanned destination through authentication and setup.

Preserve App Store compliance when simplifying registration.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested not to change anything that could cause App Store rejection.

**How to apply:** Preserve Apple sign-in, consent/age safeguards, moderation, report/block/account deletion, and privacy controls. Do not describe a private visit account as anonymous social access or promise App Review approval.
