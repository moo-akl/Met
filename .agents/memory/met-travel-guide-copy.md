Do not use the word “forever” in Met destination-guide copy.

**Why:** The user agreed that this word should not appear in travel-guide articles.

**How to apply:** Check article body text, headings, cards, and snippets when writing or revising destination guides.

Destination guides should combine must-see places, lesser-known finds, and recommendations grouped by what a traveler wants to experience. Treat this as researched editorial curation, not first-hand reporting unless the user supplies that experience.

**Why:** The user asked for destination content that helps people discover hidden gems, essential sights, and places to go for a particular interest.

**How to apply:** For each city or country, distinguish iconic sights from quieter options, label interest-led picks, and verify place details before publishing.
