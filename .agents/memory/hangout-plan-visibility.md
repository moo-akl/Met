---
name: Hangout plan visibility
description: User-approved public visibility and location policy for free-form hangout plans.
---

On Home, “Your plans” is for invite-only plans with friends; “Hangouts” is for public plans anyone can request to join. Public hangouts have no required categories. New public hangouts and changed locations must use a map-search result; the exact selected place is visible before approval. Unchanged legacy text-only locations may still be edited. Joining still requires host acceptance.

Friend-only plans may be shared outside Met through a private invite link and the native share sheet. Do not add platform-specific social integrations for this flow unless requested.

Hangouts do not use event RSVPs or check-ins: hosts do not RSVP, and there is no event QR or checked-in attendee list. Hosts and accepted members should open the Hangout chat when they tap a Hangout; people who have not been accepted still need the details page to request entry.

**Why:** The user chose public visibility and exact-place display before approval, rejected forced categories, and clarified that friend plans and public hangouts are separate Home sections. Private links let them invite friends outside Met without adding third-party social permissions.

**How to apply:** Keep friend plans separate from public hangouts in Home and preserve public discovery and exact-place visibility in hangout screens and APIs. Share outside Met through private links and the native share sheet. Keep chat access gated until the host accepts a request, omit RSVP/check-in UI for Hangouts, and do not force migration of unchanged legacy text-only locations.
