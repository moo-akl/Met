---
name: Venue prospect source verification
description: Verify each venue's contact and activity evidence independently before counting or importing a prospect.
---

For public-source prospect research, do not group evidence by the last two host labels alone. Public suffixes, subdomains, and operator domains can merge unrelated venues or attach the wrong contact and activity source. Batch-generated drafts can also select a real but irrelevant quote from the same page.

For outreach, use only email addresses explicitly published by the venue on an official source and suitable for general business contact. Do not use OSM-only addresses or role-specific addresses for hiring or artist submissions.

**Why:** A real public address may still be intended for a narrow purpose, and using it for commercial outreach can misroute the message.

**How to apply:** Before adding an email to a prospect or preparing a draft, verify it on the venue's own site or official business link page and check its stated purpose; otherwise keep the prospect held.

**Why:** A candidate inventory merged unrelated official gallery sites, and automated approach drafts selected weak or unrelated evidence.

**How to apply:** Verify each prospect's official contact and fit URLs independently, confirm the email appears on the official source, and compare the fit quote with that venue's saved page before counting or importing it.

OpenStreetMap/Overpass is a useful free source for discovering venue candidates, but map tags and contributed contact links are leads, not independent verification.

**Why:** Free search pages were blocked or unreliable during public venue research, while Overpass yielded named businesses and their listed official sites.

**How to apply:** Use OSM to find candidates, then confirm each venue's activity and a public contact route on its own official site before counting it; include OSM attribution and source links.

**Why:** Search services can be blocked or quota-limited while Nominatim and venue-owned sites remain available without paid access.

**How to apply:** When paid tools are out of scope, use Nominatim only for discovery, verify activity and contact details directly on each official site, and save an OpenStreetMap search or object link with the prospect.

Matching a prior message to a shared brand or email domain does not prove it targeted the same venue location. Keep branch-level contact history separate; when the relationship is unclear, hold the prospect for review rather than globally suppressing its contact.

**Why:** Prior outreach to one Snakes & Lattes location may be relevant to another location, but the branch-level relationship is not established by a shared domain alone.

**How to apply:** Compare venue name, location, and exact recipient. Treat another branch's message as an ambiguous brand-level match and require admin review before outreach.
