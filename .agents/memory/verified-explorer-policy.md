---
name: Verified Explorer eligibility
description: User-selected criteria for earning the Verified Explorer tier.
---

Verified Explorer eligibility is a cumulative checklist: check-ins at five
distinct venues and ten positive meeting reviews. Any recorded check-in source,
including QR, counts, but QR is not required. There is no active-week gate.

**Why:** The user selected five unique venues, ten positive meeting feedback
items, optional QR credit, and removal of the weekly requirement.

**How to apply:** Keep check-in venue counts and positive meeting reviews
separate and visible. If the criteria change, update status calculation,
response schema, profile progress, and Explorer-only event access together.
