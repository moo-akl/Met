---
name: Admin-managed venues
description: Product decision about where ownerless venues are administered.
---

Admin-added venues with no invited owner should be manageable directly in Venue Admin; do not require the administrator to create or sign in with a separate Venue Manager account.

**Why:** When offered those two approaches, the user explicitly chose management directly in Venue Admin. An uninvited venue with only a placeholder owner and no admin editing surface would not meet that expectation.

**How to apply:** Extend Venue Admin's protected management surface for future editing features on these venues. Preserve the owner-invitation path as a separate choice, and do not silently grant the placeholder owner a real manager account.
