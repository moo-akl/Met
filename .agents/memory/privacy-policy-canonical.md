The canonical Met Privacy Policy is served at `https://met-app.org/privacy` from the API server. Keep app and outreach links pointed to this route; do not redirect it to a third-party hosted copy.

**Why:** The third-party hosted policy was stale and contradicted current visibility behavior, while its source was not editable in this repository. Serving the policy at Met's own route keeps the public copy aligned with code and testable.

**How to apply:** Update the API server's public policy page and verify that onboarding, paywall, store-facing, and outreach links continue to resolve to `met-app.org/privacy`.
