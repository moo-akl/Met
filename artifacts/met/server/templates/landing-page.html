How it works

Simple by design

No swiping. No algorithm. Just the people who were actually there.

Met uses Bluetooth and GPS to silently detect other Met users nearby — at the gym, café, commute, or anywhere you go.

You appear as a silhouette. No names, no photos — until you decide to reveal. Full control, always.
