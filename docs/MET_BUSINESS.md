# Met — Competitor Map & Monetization Plan

## Product positioning

Met is a proximity-based social app. Your phone is a beacon. People you come within ~50m of are quietly saved as **encounters** — anonymous to you and you to them. Either side can send a **reveal request**; if accepted, the encounter becomes a **connection** with full profile + social handle access.

Three things make Met different from everything else in the category:

1. **Anonymous by default, mutual reveal.** Nobody sees anyone unless both sides choose.
2. **One verified photo, no swiping.** Hinge's quieter cousin.
3. **Social handles, not chat.** Met is the introduction layer; conversations move to Instagram/X/etc.

## Competitor landscape (2025)

| App | Mechanism | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Happn** | GPS, 250m crossed-paths | Closest spiritual sibling | Dating-only; identity revealed up front; weak outside big cities; ~$25M revenue |
| **Genie Connections** | Bluetooth real-time | Verified profiles, real-time | Credit model; small user base |
| **Vicinity** | GPS chat rooms | Live channels for places & events | Ephemeral; not relationship-building |
| **Bumble BFF** | GPS swipe | Brand trust, women-first | Swipe fatigue; not anonymous |
| **Patook** | Proximity + interests | Strictly platonic | Free, unclear monetization |
| **222 / Timeleft / Meet5** | Curated group dinners | High-intent IRL meetups | Event-driven, not ambient |
| **Tinder / Hinge** | Wide-radius swipe | Scale, network effects | Fatigue; Tinder revenue −4% YoY Q2 2025 |
| **Nextdoor** | Verified address | Hyper-local trust | Neighbors only |

**Direct competitors:** Happn, Genie Connections, Vicinity.
**Indirect competitors:** Bumble BFF, Hinge (for the "intentional dating" positioning).
**Tangential:** Timeleft / Meet5 / Pie (curated IRL meetup model).

### Why Met wins

- Happn shows photos immediately. Met inverts that — privacy *is* the product.
- "People you cross paths with" is broader than romance. Same mechanic, much bigger TAM.
- Social-handle exchange instead of in-app chat = lower ops cost, higher trust, you don't become another inbox.

## Monetization model

**Free tier (the beacon stays free forever):**
- Active beacon
- Encounters from the last 7 days
- 3 reveal requests per week
- 2 social handles displayed (Instagram + 1)

**Met+ subscription — $9.99/mo or $59.99/yr (~$5/mo)**

Positioned above Happn's mid-tier, well below Grindr Unlimited, in line with Bumble.

- Unlimited reveal requests
- Full encounter history
- **See who sent you a reveal request before you decide to look** — the single highest-converting feature in this category historically (Tinder Gold "see who liked you")
- All 5 social handles unlocked
- Beacon Boost — boost visibility for 30 min at events
- Read-receipts on requests
- Filter encounters (last seen <24h, ≥3 crossings, etc.)

**Met Verified — $4.99/mo standalone, bundled into Met+**

Face-match verification badge. Verified profiles get higher accept rates → organic upsell pressure.

**One-time credits (the impulse lever):**
- "Reveal Now" pack: 5 reveal requests for $2.99
- "Boost Beacon": single 30-min boost for $1.99

Captures the 30–40% of category spenders who refuse to subscribe.

**Blended ARPU at scale:** $8–10 (all users) / $35–45 (paying user) — squarely in Bumble territory and well above Happn.

## Defensibility

- **Verification as default expectation** — every catfish-driven competitor gets undercut.
- **Mutual-reveal mechanic** — Happn can't copy this without breaking their core model.
- **Low-ops product** — no in-app chat means no moderation overhead at the scale of Bumble/Tinder.
- **Network effect is hyper-local** — winning a single city creates an unassailable moat in that city, then expand outward (the Nextdoor / Bumble launch playbook).
