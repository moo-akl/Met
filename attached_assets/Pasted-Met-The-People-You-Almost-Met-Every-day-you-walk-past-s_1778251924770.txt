Met — The People You Almost Met

Every day you walk past someone you would have loved to know. The stranger reading your favorite book. The person two tables over at the coffee shop. The one who laughed at the same joke at the conference.

Met turns those near-misses into real connections  — but only when both of you decide you want to meet.

100% mutual. 100% opt-in. There is no map. There is no live location of any user. No one ever sees you unless you choose to be seen.

WHY MET FEELS DIFFERENT
• Mutual reveal only — no profile, no name, no message until both people say yes
• No map view, no live tracking — Met never plots users on a map and never shows where anyone is right now
• Verified photos on every profile so you always know who's on the other side
• Total visibility control — appear when you want, disappear in one tap, leave any chat at any time

HOW IT WORKS
1. Build a quick profile — one verified photo, one line about you
2. Open Met when you're somewhere worth meeting people — a café, a gym, a festival, a coworking space
3. Discover other Met members who chose to be visible at that place
4. Spot someone interesting? Send a reveal request. They decide
5. When they say yes, you can chat — and keep the connection in your private list of people worth remembering
