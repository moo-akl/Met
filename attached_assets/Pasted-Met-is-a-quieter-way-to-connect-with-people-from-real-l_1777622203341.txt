Met is a quieter way to connect with people from real life.

Instead of feeds, followers, and endless swiping, Met shows you the people you actually crossed paths with — at the gym, the café, the conference, the bookstore. When two people opt in to reveal themselves, the encounter becomes a real connection. No chasing. No noise.

Anonymous by default. You're invisible until you choose to be seen.

WHAT MAKES MET DIFFERENT
• Real encounters, not algorithms — see who you're genuinely sharing or shared a place with now, in the past hour or the past week
• Mutual reveal — both sides have to want to connect; nobody can DM you out of nowhere
• No feeds, no followers, no likes — Met is built for keepsakes, not engagement
• Photo verification — every profile photo is checked so you know who you're meeting
• Privacy-first — control your visibility and discovery range (10m, 50m, or 200m)

HOW IT WORKS
1. Set up a quick profile with a verified photo and a one-line bio
2. Walk through the world with Met running in the background
3. See your recent encounters — people you actually crossed paths with
4. Tap "Send reveal request" on someone you'd like to know — when they reveal back, you're connected
5. Keep your connections as quiet keepsakes, with private notes and meeting spots

GREAT FOR
• Meeting people at conferences and events without exchanging contact cards
• Reconnecting with someone you saw twice at the same coffee shop
• Building a quiet network of people from your real-world routine
• Anyone tired of dating apps and noisy social feeds

MET PLUS & MET PRO
Upgrade to Plus for unlimited encounters, full history, read receipts, frequent paths, privacy mode, and a verified badge. Met Pro adds Boost, see-who-viewed-your-profile, and a premium gold badge. Both available as monthly or annual subscriptions.

LANGUAGES
Met is available in English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Dutch, Vietnamese, Russian, Arabic, and Chinese.

YOUR PRIVACY
You're anonymous until you decide otherwise. We don't sell your data, we don't show you ads, and we don't broadcast your identity. Your encounters stay between you and the people you choose to reveal yourself to.

Met is a place for genuine encounters. We hope you find yours.
